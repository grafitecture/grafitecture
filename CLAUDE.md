# grafitecture.de – notes for Claude

Static one-pager for Grafitecture GmbH (Martin Schweppe, Diplom-Designer, Mannheim). German and English are written in one
source page (`src/body.html`); the build makes one page per language: `/` (German) and `/en` (English).

## Build and deploy

- Edit `src/body.html`, `src/styles.css`, `src/app.js`; binary assets live in `assets/`.
- `python3 build.py` (standard library only) regenerates `public/`, `vercel.json` and `seo/lastmod.json`. Vercel serves `public/` as is; there is no build step on Vercel (`package.json` holds only the check scripts and has no `build` script on purpose).
- Pages (since 05.11.2026, branch `tech-max`): `/` = `public/index.html` (German only), `/en` = `public/en.html` (English only), and Impressum/Datenschutz as pages of their own: `/impressum`, `/datenschutz`, `/en/legal-notice`, `/en/privacy-policy` (text taken from the dialogs in body.html, look of the 404 page). Addresses have no trailing slash; `PAGES` in build.py. `only_lang()` drops the elements of the other language; `lang="en-GB"` marks an English phrase inside German text (kept on `/`).
- Before every push: `npm run seo:check` must be green (build in sync, pages, sitemap, robots, CSP hashes, internal links, redirects). GitHub Actions run it with `--full` (Lighthouse mobile ≥ 95, axe 0 violations) on every push; `.github/workflows/deployed.yml` checks production after each deployment and sends IndexNow.
- Never edit `public/` by hand. Commit `public/` and `vercel.json` together with every source change.
- `dist/artifact/index.html` is the claude.ai artifact preview (font and client logos inlined, no mailto form action). It is not committed.
- Vercel project `grafitecture_webseite` (team Grafitecture) is linked to this repository; pushing to `main` deploys to production at https://www.grafitecture.de (the vercel.app URLs are behind Vercel Authentication). grafitecture.de redirects to www with 308. DNS is managed outside Vercel (nameservers a–d.ns14.net, AutoDNS): www is a CNAME to Vercel, the apex needs the A records 216.150.1.1 and 216.150.16.1. Never touch the MX records (Martin's e-mail runs on the domain).
- Privacy set-up after the note by Sebastian Enders (26.10.2026): `vercel.json` pins the function region to Frankfurt (`"regions": ["fra1"]`, set in `vercel_config()` in build.py; the site has no functions, static files come from the nearest CDN location). The team must stay on Vercel Pro (the DPA covers Pro and Enterprise; Hobby is non-commercial only). Keep Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights off; adding them or any other Vercel feature that loads a script needs the privacy policy updated first.

## Design rules (set by Martin, binding)

- Colours: #435960 and #FFFFFF, plus one accent, lime #dafd55 (`--lime`, Martin 25.10., replaced the earlier pink). No tints, no opacity-based colours. Exceptions: the two colour photos and the shimmering surface of the hero and of the field behind the Impressum/Datenschutz sheet (below).
- Lines (Martin 29.10.): every line, underline and outline is lime #dafd55 on white and white on the lime and shimmering fields. One custom property does it: `--line` (lime), which `.is-lime` sets to white; use `var(--line)` for any new line. That covers the list rules, the header line when scrolled, the active nav and language underline, the menu lines, the footer line, the contact panel lines, form field lines (also thicker on focus and error), the copy buttons, the message preview frame, the outline button in the hero, the Impressum/Datenschutz title line, close button and note line, and underlined links. Filled buttons keep their edge in their fill colour; icons (arrows, menu bars, pause) follow the type. Exception: the keyboard focus ring stays #435960, because a lime ring would be invisible on white (1.2:1).
- Lime fields (#dafd55, full width) with #435960 type and white lines: the hero (Martin 25.10.), the process section „So läuft’s“ and the contact section „Bereit?“ (Martin 26.10.). They carry the class `is-lime`. The footer is white with #435960 type and logo and a lime line, no colour and no shimmer (Martin 28.10.; it was lime from 25.10. and shimmered from 26.10.), so the lime contact field ends in a clean edge above it. No image in the hero and nothing moving in it except its surface (below); a line animation through the history of technology was tried on 26.10. and removed again at Martin's request (git history, commit 1310fc4). The header stays white.
- Surface shimmer in the hero (Martin 26.10., reworked 28.10.): only the hero field changes colour, and always exactly two colours play against each other (Martin 28.10.): four soft areas drift over the field (two per colour, drift periods 22–37 s), and where they meet the two colours mix in linear light, so no grey and no third colour appears (the share of the second colour goes through smoothstep twice, so each colour keeps large plain areas). The pair travels once around the whole colour wheel in about 72 s, the second colour running 60–100° ahead; it starts with lime and neon cyan. The wheel is variant A, chosen by Martin on 28.10.: `WHEEL` in app.js, 36 colours 10° apart in OKLCH hue, each the most saturated colour of its hue on which #435960 keeps at least 4.6:1, made by `tools/colour_wheel.py`. So yellow, green and cyan are full neon, red, pink, violet and blue light neon tones; with mixing, rounding and dither the type never drops below 4.5:1 (WCAG AA; it was 5.5:1 before 28.10.). Fully saturated red, pink, violet or blue would make the type unreadable (down to 1:1); Martin chose against that. Speed: 38 s per colour step on 26.10., `SPEED = 1.6` on 27.10., `SPEED = 4` on 28.10. (Martin: faster). `sheen()` paints the field on a canvas `.sheen` at a sixth of the resolution (12 updates a second, ordered dither), the browser scales it up; it fades in from plain lime over 4 s when it first comes into view. „So läuft’s“ and „Bereit?“ stay plain lime. Without JavaScript and with reduced motion the hero is plain lime. A small pause icon sits next to the hero kicker (label visually hidden, WCAG 2.2.2). The field rests while it is out of view or the tab is hidden. The footer shimmered too from 26.10. (57 s ahead of the hero, fading out over its top half, with a second pause icon); Martin had it removed on 28.10. The figures section (26 / 27 / 24/7) was removed on 26.10.; „Referenzen“ follows „So läuft’s“ with its normal top padding.
- Shimmer behind Impressum and Datenschutz (Martin 29.10., replaced the plain #435960 backdrop): each legal dialog covers the whole viewport (lime base, `::backdrop` transparent) with the white sheet `.legal-in` on the right (820 px wide, it scrolls itself, sticky title) and a `.sheen` canvas beside it, painted by the same `sheen()` as the hero. Hero and dialogs share one timeline (`clock` in app.js): a dialog opens in the colours the hero shows, and the hero carries on from there when it closes; only the visible field runs (the hero rests while a dialog is open, a dialog canvas only while it is shown). The field and the sheet fade in over .45 s, the sheet slides in 40 px. Each dialog has its own pause icon in the top left corner of the field; every pause icon pauses all fields. A click on the field closes the dialog (only if it was also pressed there, so ending a text selection beside the sheet does not). Up to 820 px the sheet fills the screen and the canvas and its icon are hidden, so nothing runs. With reduced motion the field is plain lime. On opening, focus goes to „Schließen“ (the sheet is a scroll area and would otherwise take it).
- The accent is always the full tone #dafd55: never a darker or lighter variant, no tint, no transparency. Text on a lime field is always #435960 (6.4:1), never white.
- Nothing is put behind type or logos (Martin 25.10.): no marker, no underlay. Martin chose lime type knowing it is only 1.2:1 on white.
- All main headlines are plain #435960 like the rest of the type (hero H1, the section headlines, the Impressum/Datenschutz titles; Martin 25.10., after trying pink and lime). Nothing moves in them: the lime wave that ran through the headlines on white from 25.10. was removed on 28.10. at Martin's request (git history).
- Every mouse-over on the whole page is lime, and nothing is put behind type or logos (Martin 25.10.): on white the type itself turns #dafd55 (links, nav, menu, e-mail, Impressum/Datenschutz, language and menu buttons) and the header and footer logo turn #dafd55. Martin chose this knowing lime type on white is only 1.2:1 while hovered. Buttons, copy and close buttons fill lime with #435960 type; client logos get a smaller lime field and stay #435960. On the lime fields (`.is-lime`), where lime cannot show, the type turns white and buttons (incl. the copy button) fill white with #435960 type. The e-mail address and the Impressum/Datenschutz links are not underlined. All hover rules sit in one `@media (hover: hover)` block, so taps on touch screens leave nothing behind.
- Font: Google Sans, self-hosted (`assets/fonts`, Latin subset, weight 400–700).
- Type scale, golden ratio with clamp, maximum at 1920 px: h1 17.942rem, h2 11.089rem, h3 6.854rem, h4 4.236rem, h5 2.618rem, h6 1.618rem, p 1rem, small 0.618rem, 0.382rem.
- Calm and simple: large white space, large headlines, no city-map or floor-plan motifs.
- Logo: `assets/logo/grafitecture.svg` in the header and in the white footer (`grafitecture-white.svg` is no longer used). Both always the same size (`--logo-h`).
- The H1 always breaks after the first word: "Marken / mit KI.", "Brands / with AI." (explicit `<br>`), also in the OG image.

Mehr Gestaltung.“ never wraps (`white-space: nowrap`, below 380 px with letter-spacing .1em instead of .16em so it fits next to the pause icon down to 320 px). ## Content rules

- Every visible text exists twice: `<span lang="de">…</span><span lang="en">…</span>`.
- `body.html` placeholders filled by build.py: `{{LOGO_DARK}}`, `{{LOGO_WHITE}}`, `{{ARROW}}`, `{{CLIENTS}}`, `{{CLIENT_COUNT}}` (currently unused), `{{IMG_PORTRAIT}}`, `{{IMG_DOOR}}`. Client names, order and links: `CLIENTS` in build.py.
- Impressum and Datenschutz are dialogs in body.html (status October 2026: § 5 DDG, § 18 Abs. 2 MStV, hosting Vercel), and since 05.11.2026 also pages built from the same text (see Build and deploy); footer and menu link to the pages, and on `/` and `/en` a click still opens the dialog (`data-open`). Keep the privacy policy short, and update it whenever a cookie, analytics, external font or other third-party service is added.
- No cookies, no tracking, no third-party requests; the CSP in vercel.json allows only 'self' (scripts by hash).
- Title and description stay as Martin wrote them (05.11.2026, longer than the ~60/160 characters Google shows; seo_check.py only warns).
- Instagram (Martin 01.11.2026): its own footer column „Social“ (the same label in DE and EN) with a plain text link „Instagram“ (https://www.instagram.com/g.r.a.f.i.t.e.c.t.u.r.e/, new tab), plus `sameAs` in the JSON-LD. Footer columns: Büro, Kontakt, Social, Rechtliches; one column on phones, two by two from 760 px, four from 1300 px (below that the e-mail address would wrap in a quarter column). No icon, no embed, nothing loaded from Instagram; the privacy policy (point 4) says so in one sentence.

## SEO- & Technik-Standards (nicht verhandelbar)

- Keine URL ändern oder löschen ohne Zeile in `seo/redirect-map.csv` und funktionierende Weiterleitung (`npm run seo:redirects`).
- Jede neue Seite bekommt: Title, Description, genau eine H1, Canonical, hreflang, Open Graph, JSON-LD, interne Links; Eintrag in `PAGES` in build.py, dann erscheint sie automatisch in der Sitemap.
- Bilder immer mit `alt`, `width`/`height` und modernem Format (AVIF mit JPEG-Rückfall).
- Inhalte immer serverseitig im HTML, keine rein clientseitig gerenderten Inhalte; jeder Text in beiden Sprachen (`<span lang="de">…</span><span lang="en">…</span>`).
- Keine neuen Drittanbieter-Dienste ohne Consent-Prüfung und Anpassung der Datenschutzerklärung (CSP erlaubt nur `'self'`).
- Kein `noindex` und kein `Disallow: /` auf Produktion, keine Weiterleitung aller unbekannten Adressen auf die Startseite.
- Vor jedem Push: `npm run seo:check` grün.
