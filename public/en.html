I build AI that works. And looks good doing it.

Since 2000 I have been designing brands with Grafitecture in Mannheim: first on paper, then on the web, today with artificial intelligence. Twenty-six years have taught me that the bottleneck is rarely the idea. It is the execution: recurring workflows, data upkeep and content production that tie up entire teams.

Today I design and run systems that take over these workflows entirely. From AI agents that work around the clock to websites that go live in weeks. With a designer’s standards: everything I build has to work and fit the brand.

My goal is simple: to give business owners their time back. Whether it’s an agent system, an AI-powered product or a content engine, it has to run in real operations, not just in a demo.

  • Graduate designer (Dipl.-Des.) and entrepreneur since 2000
  • Clients across healthcare, hospitality, finance and logistics
  • Print, web and AI from a single studio
